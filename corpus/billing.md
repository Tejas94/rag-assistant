# Billing and plans

## Plans

Northwind Cloud has three plans. **Starter** costs £12 per month and includes 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM and 50 GB of SSD storage. **Team** costs £49 per month and includes 8 vCPUs, 16 GB of RAM, 250 GB of storage and up to 10 seats. **Enterprise** is priced per contract and adds a 99.99% uptime SLA, single sign-on and a named support engineer.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are payable within 14 days. Customers on the Team plan and above can pay by bank transfer; Starter customers must pay by card. VAT is added for UK customers at the standard rate.

## Overages

Bandwidth above the plan allowance is billed at £0.05 per GB. Storage overages are billed at £0.08 per GB per month, measured as the daily peak averaged over the month.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.
