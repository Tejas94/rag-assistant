# Support

## Channels

Starter customers get email support with a first response within 2 business days. Team customers get email and chat support with a first response within 4 business hours. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support with a 30-minute response for severity 1 incidents.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means production is down for all users. Severity 2 means a major feature is broken or production is degraded. Severity 3 covers everything else, including questions.

## Status page

Live incident updates are published at status.northwind.example. You can subscribe by email or webhook.

## Office hours

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 UK time, Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.
